About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in Software Engineering at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Beihang University, advised by Prof. Wenfei Fan (CAS Academician) and Assoc. Prof. Ping Lu.

My research centers on Data-Centric AI, with a focus on Data Quality, Knowledge Discovery, and Model Reliability in graph learning. My work spans entity alignment, data augmentation, graph association rule mining, and GNN explainability, while also exploring the use of LLMs for data processing and knowledge discovery.
